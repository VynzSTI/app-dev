# Fury (2014)

## A. Introduction

**Fury** is a war and action-drama movie released in 2014, directed by David Ayer. The movie takes place during the final months of World War II and follows a group of American soldiers inside a Sherman tank. It shows their struggles, teamwork, friendship, and sacrifices during the war.

**Genre:** War, Action, Drama  
**Release Year:** 2014  
**Director:** David Ayer

## B. Synopsis

The story takes place in April 1945, near the end of World War II. It follows Sergeant Don "Wardaddy" Collier and his five-man tank crew as they carry out dangerous missions across Germany.

Their crew becomes more complicated when a young soldier named Norman Ellison joins them as a replacement. Norman has little experience in combat, making it difficult for him to adjust to the harsh reality of war.

As they continue their mission, the crew faces difficult situations that test their courage and teamwork. Despite their differences, they learn to depend on one another as they face the challenges ahead.

The movie shows how war affects soldiers and how strong their bond can become after going through difficult experiences together.

## C. Characters

Here are five important characters in the movie:

1. **Don "Wardaddy" Collier (Brad Pitt)** – The commander of the Fury tank crew. He is experienced, strict, and protective of his men.
2. **Boyd "Bible" Swan (Shia LaBeouf)** – The tank's gunner. He is religious and often relies on his faith.
3. **Norman Ellison (Logan Lerman)** – A young soldier who joins the crew with very little combat experience.
4. **Trini "Gordo" Garcia (Michael Peña)** – The driver of the Fury tank. He is loyal and dependable.
5. **Grady "Coon-Ass" Travis (Jon Bernthal)** – The tank's mechanic and loader. He has a tough personality and is an important member of the crew.

## D. Personal Review

I chose this movie because I enjoy watching military machinery such as tanks, especially those that show teamwork and the experiences of soldiers. Brad Pitt also did a great job portraying Wardaddy.

Three things I liked about the movie are:

- **The acting:** The actors did a great job making their characters believable, especially the character Norman.
- **The story:** It shows the struggles of how being a soldier let alone a tank crew is in the war.
- **The cinematography:** The scenes and visuals make the movie have a feeling of dread and realism.

**My Rating: 9.7/10**

I would recommend this movie to people who enjoy military movies or something that tells how brutal war really is and how much sacrifices it takes to get freedom.
